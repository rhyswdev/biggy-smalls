# KB0009871: Checkout latency triage

Space: COM · Owner: Commerce Platform · Last updated 9 Mar 2026 by Alice Nguyen

Written after INC0042041 (spring campaign, checkout slow and failing at payment). Use when checkout is slow or erroring at submit and the "checkout-api error rate" or "p90 latency" monitors fire.

## First five minutes

1. Check whether traffic is up. Compare `trace.servlet.request.hits` for checkout-api against the same hour last week. Campaign emails and app pushes are the usual cause.
2. Check checkout-api's connection pool. Hikari logs pool stats at DEBUG (`After cleanup  stats`). If `active` is at the pool maximum with `waiting` above zero on most pods, requests are queueing behind the pool.
3. Check checkout-db. If queries are fast and the database is quiet while the pool is full, the pool is the bottleneck, not the database.

## Mitigate

- Scale checkout-api out: `kubectl -n commerce scale deploy/checkout-api --replicas=12`. The HPA will take over again within the hour.
- If pods stay saturated after scaling, raise the checkout-api pool through a standard change (it was raised from 20 to 40 under CHG0029514).
- If a deploy went out in the last hour, roll it back first (KB0010233).

## After

- Post the traffic graph and pool stats in the incident channel.
- Record in the incident which mitigation worked.
