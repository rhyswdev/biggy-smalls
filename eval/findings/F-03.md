# F-03 · prompt · reasoning · seen in most runs reviewed

**What happened.** A hypothesis was taken to the bottom of the confidence scale, with several rows in `against` and none of its own in `support`, and then left `open` with nothing said about why. The report shows the incident commander an explanation that is neither cleared nor pursued. The reviewer marks the statement unsupported, and no open question says what would still decide it.

**What the method says.** When evidence clears a hypothesis, set it `rejected` and put the rows that clear it in `against`. The others stay open until evidence supports or clears them, and what is still unknown belongs in `open_questions` as something a person or a call could answer.

**Fixed when.** Every hypothesis left `open` in the report is either still credible (it has support of its own) or is named in an open question that says what check would decide it. A hypothesis the evidence has cleared is `rejected`. The prompt still allows an honest "not yet": it neither forces a rejection nor sets a confidence that triggers one.

**Keep.** Keep what works, including the two earlier passes' outcomes (HISTORY.md), the stance that an honest "not yet" beats a forced conclusion, and the readiness rule that every other `cause` needs a row in `against`.
