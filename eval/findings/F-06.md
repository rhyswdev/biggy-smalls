# F-06 · harness and prompt · method · a quiet board is nudged, not refused

**What happened.** Twice in runs driven by hand, the agent read for three rounds without writing to the board, before its first write or between two writes. The harness then refused every read of the next round because the board had not changed. That cost a model turn and showed the commander refused calls. One of the quiet rounds held nothing but calls with bad arguments, and the count also ran while the board was still empty. Both are likelier now that the agent chooses its own first reads.

**What changed in the harness.** The harness no longer refuses reads for a quiet board. When nothing on the board has changed for three rounds, empty or not, the status line at the end of the round carries one more part, just before the budget: `the board has not changed in <n> rounds: record what the evidence shows with update_board, or submit`. It appears in every round until the board moves, and nothing is refused. Only the model sees it; the commander does not. A round in which every call had bad arguments does not count. The budget still refuses reads once it is spent, with "submit now".

**What the files still say that is no longer true.** The system prompt's description of the status line lists its parts without this one. Its stale rule says that after a few rounds that move nothing, "the gate refuses reads until you record what the evidence shows or submit".

**Fixed when.**
- The status-line description includes the new part, in its place before the budget, and says what it asks for.
- Nothing says that a quiet board makes the gate refuse reads.
- The prompt says what to do when the nudge appears: record on the board what the reads so far show (a new hypothesis, a change of confidence, a rejection, a support or against row), or submit.

**Keep.** Everything else: the method, the board and its rules, the stale flag on single hypotheses, the citation rules, the stopping rule, the agent choosing where to start, the direct answer to the commander's request, and the outcomes of the earlier passes (HISTORY.md). Pass 1 showed that wording about when to send board updates does not change how the model groups them, so this pass does not restate that rule.
