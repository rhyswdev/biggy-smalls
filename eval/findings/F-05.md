# F-05 · harness and prompt · method · the agent chooses its own first reads

**What changed in the harness.** The harness no longer reads anything before the agent's first turn. Until now it ran a fixed opening: the alert streams and the changes around the trigger, and the recent chat, delivered in one message after the first turn. That opening assumed that alerting covers the fault and is working, and it often does not. A service may never have been wired to an alert. The alerting system may itself be down, muted or late. A fault can show only in logs, in metrics, in tickets or in what people say. From now on the first turn holds only the clock, the source inventory, the trigger, the budget and any earlier board, and the agent decides what to read first.

**What the files still say that is no longer true.** The system prompt and the first-turn template tell the agent that the harness runs an opening sweep and hands it the results, and to start from them; one board rule says to write the first hypotheses "from the sweep and the trigger". The report's alert-verdict field asks for a verdict on every alert stream "the opening sweep listed".

**What the harness does now about alert verdicts.** The report must give a verdict for every alert stream that any of the agent's own `list_alerts` calls showed. A report that misses some is sent back once with the list; after that, code records the rest as unassessed. A run that never lists alerts owes no verdicts.

**Fixed when.**
- The prompt and the first-turn template say that the agent decides where to start, from the trigger and the inventory, and that its first turn may hold several independent reads at once.
- They say, in generic terms, how to choose a first look. What is alerting, what changed and what the people on the incident are saying are often useful starting points, but none of them is assumed. The absence of an alert is not evidence that nothing is wrong: monitoring can be incomplete, broken or late, and logs, metrics, changes, tickets and chat can show what alerting does not.
- The alert-verdict text asks for a verdict on every alert stream the agent's own alert listings showed, and on any other it assessed.
- Nothing refers to an opening sweep or to results the harness supplies.

**Keep.** Everything else: the method, the board and its rules, the citation rules, the stopping rule, the direct answer to the commander's request, and the outcomes of the earlier passes (HISTORY.md).
