# F-08 · prompt · reasoning · seen in a minority of recent runs

**What happened.** A condition that the leading hypothesis's own account describes as one of its effects was also kept on the board as a separate open `cause`, at a middle confidence. The report then offers two causes where its reasoning has one cause and one of its effects.

**What the method says.** A rival cause is one that would explain the symptoms differently. An effect of the leading cause belongs to that hypothesis's account: as support, as a `symptom`, or as `contributing` if it made things worse in its own right. A condition stands as a rival cause only with evidence that it arose on its own.

**Fixed when.** No run keeps, as an open `cause`, a condition that its leading hypothesis describes as an effect.

**Keep.** Stated causes still go on the board and are tested (passes 3 and 4); rivals are still written early; the citation rules (pass 2), the quiet-board rule, and everything else. Add no rule about when to send board updates (pass 1).
