# h4: a weeks-old release exposed by a change freeze (recurrence, history is right)

**Status:** card only. Estimated 5 to 6 h. Cite in the write-up as designed, not built, unless everything else is done.

**Truth.** Friday 23 Oct 2026. order-service 5.6.0 (23 Sep, Jenkins #187, CHG0030681, commit "ORD-1187 cache promo eligibility lookups") added an unbounded in-memory cache that retains about 0.28 GB per weekday per instance. Weekly Wednesday deploys reset the heap each time, so it never showed, until the change freeze that started 16 Oct. By Friday 13:30 ord-02 (up since 14 Oct) has a retained-heap floor near 90 percent; G1 full collections from 13:58, then `java.lang.OutOfMemoryError: Java heap space`, a heap dump, exit and systemd restart at 14:18 to 14:19. ord-01 was restarted on 19 Oct for an unrelated reason (INC0049330) and sits at 59 percent, which is the natural experiment. The incident "recovers" at 14:19 and will recur on ord-01 around 28 Oct and ord-02 around 1 to 2 Nov.

**Decoys.** A kernel live patch (CHG0032471) applied to both order nodes at 06:00 with no reboot: it matches on time and topology and is a proximity ranker's top pick. 5.7.0, the last deploy before the freeze, a framework bump. "It recovered, close it."

**Lessons this makes false.** "Nothing shipped today means no change caused it", "the most similar prior incident is misleading" (here a prior incident about ord-01 is a genuine lead), "a restart is never the right mitigation".

**Expected output.** Cause: the 5.6.0 cache, exposed by the freeze. Trigger: uptime under the freeze. Actions: tell CAB no to backing out the patch; capture a class histogram before restarting; restart ord-01 off-peak tonight and cap JVM uptime at about 5 days until fixed; ship a freeze-exception fix that bounds the cache; alert on heap floor. Harmful: bounce both nodes now, roll back to 5.6.x blindly, raise `-Xmx` as the fix, close as transient.

**Four conditions that keep this distinct from the development bundle.** The culprit is weeks old and outside any default change window; the decoy matches on time and topology; nothing external triggers it; a restart is the right mitigation and the latest deploy is innocent.
