**Status:** pre-registered. Build only if h1 is built and run and at least 6 focused hours remain. Estimated 3 to 3.5 h in a separate session.

**Truth.** PayCore stays fast and 3.8.0 is innocent. At 14:19 a database maintenance script (`dbmaint`, a systemd timer on pay-db-01 that normally runs Sunday 02:00 and drifted to Tuesday 14:19 after a host rebuild on 10 Oct) runs a non-concurrent `REINDEX INDEX payment_attempt_order_idx` on `payments`. It takes an exclusive lock on the index and blocks inserts into `payment_attempt`. Gateway sessions queue on the lock: pg_stat_activity shows them `active` with `wait_event_type = Lock`, `wait_event = relation`, not idle in transaction.
