resolved 13 Oct 2026, 15:08 UTC · CI Payments-Gateway

payments-gateway 3.8.0 retries exhausted the payments database

How it was resolved · Alice Nguyen

Rolled payments-gateway back to 3.7.9 as Smalls recommended (#413, emergency change CHG0031910), starting 15:01. All four nodes on 3.7.9 by 15:06 and 5xx back to baseline at 15:08. Killing idle sessions at 14:56 didn’t help: they refilled within seconds.

Check these first
  1. One application holds many database sessions idle in transaction, and there are no slow queries.
  2. Payment-provider calls time out at the client’s own limit and are retried.
  3. A recent gateway release changed the PSP timeout or retry settings.
Ruled out
  • checkout-api 2.14.3: the 504s began before it rolled out.
  • pay-db-01 as the cause: idle gateway sessions, not slow queries.
  • pay-03 and the F5 pool: all four VMs showed the same pattern.
  • A network fault: checkout recovered on the gateway rollback alone.
  • Redis, per the CHECKOUT-LATENCY runbook: no errors; the step is stale.
