The detail

payments-gateway 3.8.0, released at 10:19 UTC, retried slow PayCore authorizations up to three times at 8 s each while holding a database transaction open. When PayCore slowed from about 14:26, gateway sessions sat idle in transaction and pay-db-01 ran out of connections. About 1 in 6 payments failed or hung until the rollback to 3.7.9 (15:01–15:06), and errors were at baseline by 15:08.
